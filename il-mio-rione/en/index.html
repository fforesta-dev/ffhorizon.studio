Il Mio Rione

A free, unofficial app for members of the wards and branches of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints: the notices and the calendar of activities of your ward or branch, all in one place.

  • Free, with no advertising and no purchases.
  • Everyone sees only the content of their own ward or branch, and only the content meant for them.
  • Large, easy-to-read text.

It only works in the wards and branches that use it. There is no open sign-up: if your ward or branch uses Il Mio Rione, ask the bishopric (or the branch presidency) to add you, in person or by writing from your own email to hello@ffhorizon.studio with the name of your ward or branch.

You will receive a temporary password, in person or by a message sent only to you (for example on WhatsApp or by SMS). It is valid for 7 days: sign in soon, and change it to your own the first time you sign in.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot your password?": you will receive an email with an 8-digit code, valid for one hour, that lets you choose a new password. If that doesn't work, ask the bishopric or the secretary of your ward (or the branch presidency) for a new temporary password.

My temporary password no longer works

The temporary password is valid for 7 days. If it has expired, tap "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen, or ask the bishopric (or the branch presidency) for a new temporary password.

I don't have an account

The app is only for members of the wards and branches that use it. Ask the bishopric or the branch presidency to add you, in person or by writing from your email to hello@ffhorizon.studio.

My ward or branch doesn't use the app

Then there is no point in downloading it yet: Il Mio Rione only shows the notices and activities of your own ward or branch, and each unit starts using it with the approval of its leaders. If you would like to use it, talk to the bishopric (or the branch presidency): they can write to us at hello@ffhorizon.studio.

I have moved to another ward

Let an administrator of the ward or branch you are leaving know: they will suspend your access (after 12 months the account is deleted automatically) or delete the account right away. You can also delete it yourself from the app. If your new ward or branch uses the app, ask there to be added: you will get a new account.

I am under 18

You can use the app from January of the year in which you turn 12, when you join the Young Women or the deacons quorum, with the approval of a parent (from their app) and of an administrator. Your parent must already have an account in the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → "Delete my account". The other options are on the Delete your account page.

Is this an app of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints?

No. It is an unofficial app, created to help wards and branches communicate, with the approval of local leaders. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints does not run it and does not receive the data. For the official apps (Gospel Library, Member Tools), use the Apple and Google app stores.

Contact

For problems with the app or questions about privacy, write to hello@ffhorizon.studio. For access and for the content of your ward or branch, contact your leaders.
