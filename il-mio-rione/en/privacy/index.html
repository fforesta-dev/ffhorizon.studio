Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This is a translation. If there are any differences, the Italian version prevails.

Il Mio Rione is a free, unofficial app for members of the wards and branches of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It is not an app of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, which does not run it and does not receive the data collected by the app.

In short

  • We use your data only to make the app work in your ward or branch.
  • The app is optional: you can take part in all ward activities even without using it.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics, no selling of data.
  • The data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Other members do not see your data: it is seen by the administrators of your ward or branch and, if you reply to an event, by whoever organizes it; if you offer to help with a help request, by whoever manages it.
  • Data that is no longer needed is deleted automatically, for example events after 12 months.
  • You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data, and you can delete your account directly from the app.

1. Who processes your data

The app is run by Francesco Foresta (FF Horizon Studio) together with the bishop or branch president of your ward or branch, who are joint controllers (Art. 26 of the GDPR). The platform manager develops the app, chooses the providers, collects consent, answers requests and handles any data breaches; the bishop or branch president decides who joins the app in their ward or branch, who administers it and what is published.

For any request, write to hello@ffhorizon.studio; you can also speak to your bishop or branch president. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints does not take part and does not receive the data.

2. What data we process

  • Account data: first name, last name, email address and password. The password is stored in encrypted form: no one can read it, not even the people who run the app. If you forget it, you can ask in the app for a code by email to choose a new one; when your password changes, you receive an email letting you know.
  • The ward or branch you belong to and your location (the main location or a group, for example the Viterbo Group).
  • Role in the app (member, leader or administrator) and, for leaders, the organizations they can publish for (for example the Primary).
  • The groups you belong to, for example Relief Society, Young Men or Primary. They are assigned by the administrators, to show you the content that concerns you. They may reveal gender, age and marital status.
  • If you are under 18: this information, your year of birth, the link to your parent's account and the date on which your parent gave consent or approval (see section 8).
  • If you are an administrator, bishop or branch president: who designated you as an administrator and when, and the date and version of the joint controller agreement or of the instructions for administrators that you accepted in the app.
  • What you do in the app: the notices and events you publish (with your name as the author), your "I'm going / Maybe / I'm not going" responses to events and the reports of inappropriate content you send. A report also keeps the title and text of the reported content, as they were at that moment.
  • Sacrament meeting: if you volunteer to say a prayer or give a talk, your offer (prayer or talk, the Sundays you choose or "whenever needed", an optional note and the date); also the history of the Sundays on which you prayed or spoke, which the bishopric (in branches, the branch presidency) keeps so that everyone can take a turn, including when you had not volunteered in the app. In the note, write only what is needed to make arrangements. If you don't want to appear in the history, ask the bishopric or write to hello@ffhorizon.studio: your entries are deleted and your account stays.
  • Helping each other (adults only): if you tap "I can help" on a help request, your reply, the optional message you write (for example how to contact you) and the date. If you are an administrator or leader, the help requests you write (type of help, title, text and location), who approved them and the dates. In requests and messages, write only what is needed to make arrangements: nothing about health or money.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on: a code for your phone that is needed to send them to you, the type of phone (iPhone or Android), your choices about which ones to receive (Profile → Notifications) and, for a short time, the list of notifications waiting to be sent.
  • App language (Italian, Portuguese, French, Spanish or English): the one you choose in Profile → Language or, if you don't choose one, your phone's language. It is used only to send you notifications and emails (for example the code for a new password) in your language.
  • Technical data: the date on which you consented to this policy (and which version you read) and accepted the rules of use; if your account is suspended, the date since when; the access data that our providers record for security purposes (for example IP address and time of requests). On your phone, only the data needed to keep you signed in is kept.

In the future the app may also ask for: phone number and birthday (optional: you decide whether to enter them and who can see them, and they are never shown between adults and minors). Before collecting them, we will update this policy and tell you in the app.

The app does not collect your location, contacts or photos, and does not import data from the systems of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (such as Member Tools or LCR): all data is entered by you or by an administrator of your ward or branch.

3. A special kind of data: religious affiliation

The mere fact that you use the app shows that you belong to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), this is a special category of personal data (Art. 9). For this reason, we process your data only with your explicit consent, which we ask for the first time you sign in, and only the data that is strictly necessary.

The app is optional: you can take part in all ward activities even without using it, and saying no has no consequences. If you do not consent, you can delete your account right away from the app; otherwise it is deleted automatically after 30 days.

Before you first sign in: your account is created by an administrator at your request, with your first name, last name, email, ward or branch and groups. Until you first sign in, this data is used only to give you access, on the basis of your request (Art. 6(1)(b) of the GDPR) and of the explicit consent you confirm the first time you sign in; if you don't sign in, it is deleted after 30 days.

4. Why we use the data and on what legal basis

  • Making the app work: your account, notices, the calendar, offers for sacrament meeting, help requests and notifications. Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) of the GDPR).
  • Enforcing the rules of use: reviewing reports, removing inappropriate content and suspending people who do not follow the rules. Legal basis: the legitimate interest in keeping the app safe and suitable for young people (Art. 6(1)(f) of the GDPR); for religious affiliation, explicit consent still applies, and closed reports are kept for 12 months to defend a legal claim (Art. 9(2)(f) of the GDPR).
  • Support: answering your requests and solving problems. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) of the GDPR) and, for requests about your rights, a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) of the GDPR).
  • Security: protecting the app from unauthorized access and abuse, using technical access data. Legal basis: the legitimate interest in keeping the app secure (Art. 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

We do not make automated decisions about you and we do not create profiles.

5. Who can see your data

  • The other members of your ward or branch, including leaders, do not see your data or a list of people: they only see notices, events and help requests, without the name of the person who published them.
  • The administrators of your ward or branch (the bishopric or branch presidency and the secretaries): all your account data, including email, groups and account status, and who published each notice and event, in order to manage the ward or branch. Only you and they can see your groups.
  • The organizers of an event (the administrators and the leaders of the organization that published it) see the first name, last name and response of everyone who responded. Everyone else only sees how many people are going.
  • Your offers for sacrament meeting and your history can be seen only by you and the administrators of your ward or branch, who contact you in person, by phone or by message to make arrangements. Leaders and other members cannot see them, and there is no Sunday program with names in the app.
  • If you offer to help with a help request ("I can help"), your name and your message can be seen only by those who manage the request (the administrators of your ward or branch and the leaders of the organization that published it), who contact you in person, by phone or by message. Other members only see how many people have offered. Leaders' requests are published by an administrator, who can correct the text; people under 18 do not see help requests.
  • If you report a notice, an event or a help request, the administrators of your ward or branch see the report, the reason and your name in the app. They also receive an email alert, as does the platform manager, which only says that a report has been received about a notice, an event or a help request of the ward or branch: it does not contain the content, the reason or your name. Other members, including the person who published the content, do not know who reported it.
  • If you are under 18, the parent linked to your account sees your account in order to give consent or approval, and to withdraw them.
  • The platform manager (Francesco Foresta, joint controller): to provide support, the platform manager can access the data of all wards and branches, including personal data. Only those who need access have it, and only for this purpose.

Each ward or branch sees only its own data. Your data is not sold or passed on to anyone.

6. The providers who help us

Some technical services are entrusted to external providers, who process the data only on our behalf (data processors):

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd, Singapore, with Supabase, Inc., United States): database and sign-in with email and password. The data is stored on servers in the European Union, in Frankfurt (Germany).
  • Hostinger (Hostinger International Ltd., European Union): the email mailboxes of the ffhorizon.studio domain, from which the report alert emails, the codes to choose a new password and the alert when your password changes are sent, and which receive the messages you write to us.

This website (privacy policy, rules of use and support pages) is hosted by GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States), which records visitors' IP addresses for its own security purposes as an independent controller, according to its privacy statement. This also happens when you open these pages from the app. The site does not use cookies or analytics.

Google Maps: if you tap "Open in Google Maps" in an event, the address of the event opens in Google Maps, which processes it according to Google's privacy policy. If you don't tap it, nothing is sent to Google.

Phone calendar: if you tap "Add to calendar" in an event, the title, date, place and description of the event open in your phone's calendar. If you save them, they stay there and are processed by your calendar (for example Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, with your account's sync, if it is turned on). The app does not read your calendar and does not write to it by itself; if the event later changes or is canceled, you need to correct it in the calendar yourself.

Notifications, if you turn them on: they are sent through Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States), as a data processor, which receives the phone code and a generic text, without titles or names (for example "New notice"): you see the details when you open the app. Expo also uses, as an independent controller, technical data about the device (IP address, app errors) for aggregate statistics, according to its own privacy policy. The notifications are delivered to your phone by Apple (on iPhones) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, on Android phones).

Transfers outside the European Union: Supabase Pte. Ltd (Singapore) and Supabase, Inc. (United States) can access the data, which stays in Frankfurt, only for management and support, with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses included in the contract with Supabase. Expo, Apple and Google (United States) receive only the phone code and the text of the notification: Expo and Google under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Apple under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask for a copy of the safeguards at hello@ffhorizon.studio.

7. How long we keep the data

Every night the server automatically deletes data that is no longer needed:

  • Your account stays as long as you use it. When it is deleted, we erase your personal data: the notices and events you published for the ward or branch remain, but without your name.
  • Suspended accounts (for example of people who have moved): they are deleted 12 months after the suspension.
  • Accounts never activated (never signed in to, or signed in to without giving consent): they are deleted 30 days after they were created; if you do not consent, you can delete yours right away from the app.
  • Young people aged 14 or over without their parent's approval: if the parent withdraws it or answers "I do not approve", the account is closed and deleted after 7 days, unless the parent approves it in the meantime.
  • Past events, with the "I'm going / Maybe / I'm not going" responses: they are deleted 12 months after the event.
  • Notices: those with an expiry date are deleted 6 months after they expire, the others 12 months after they were last edited. "Pinned" notices stay until an administrator removes them.
  • Reports: they remain as long as the reported content exists; resolved reports are deleted 12 months after they were resolved.
  • Sacrament meeting: offers are deleted when you withdraw them or when an administrator deletes them; if an administrator marks them as done, they move to the history. An offer that is still open is in any case deleted 3 months after the last Sunday you indicated, or 12 months after it was sent if you chose "whenever needed". The history is deleted 12 months after the Sunday.
  • Helping each other: a request marked as resolved is deleted, together with the replies, 30 days later; a request left unchanged for 12 months is deleted anyway. You can withdraw your reply whenever you want.
  • Notifications: the phone code is deleted when you sign out of the app or delete your account, and in any case if you don't open the app for 6 months; the list of notifications waiting to be sent is deleted after 30 days.
  • Agreement and designations: acceptances of the joint controller agreement and of the instructions for administrators, together with who designated each administrator, are kept for as long as the ward or branch uses the app and then for 10 years, even if the account is deleted, so that it can be shown who had which responsibilities.
  • Messages you write to us for support: they are deleted 12 months after the request is closed; requests about your rights (for example to see or delete your data) 24 months after, so that we can prove we replied.
  • Technical access data and backup copies are deleted automatically by the providers, according to their technical schedules.

If you move, an administrator can suspend your access or delete your account; you can also delete it yourself (see how to delete your account).

8. Young people under 18

  • The account of someone under 18 is activated only with two approvals: that of an administrator, who creates the account, and that of a parent or guardian, from their own app. The parent must already have an account in the app, with consent given.
  • Before giving consent, young people and parents read in the app a short privacy notice written for them.
  • Under 14, consent is given by the parent or guardian; from 14, it is given by the young person, and we still also ask for the parent's approval until 18. The app records only the year of birth, in order to ask for consent again at 14 and at 18.
  • The parent or guardian can withdraw consent at any time in the app (Profile → Your children): under 14, the child's account is deleted right away. From 14, the parent withdraws their approval: the account is closed, the young person receives a notice and the account is deleted after 7 days, unless the parent approves it again in the meantime.
  • Minors see notices and the calendar and can volunteer to say a prayer or give a talk in sacrament meeting (only the bishopric or the branch presidency sees the offer), but they do not see help requests and have no direct contact with adults: there are no chats or private messages in the app.
  • The app is not intended for Primary children: young people can have it from January of the year in which they turn 12, when they join the Young Women or the deacons quorum.

9. Your rights

At any time you can ask to:

  • know what data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict its use or object to its processing;
  • receive it in a machine-readable format (portability);
  • withdraw your consent: withdrawal does not make what was done before unlawful, but without consent you will no longer be able to use the app.

You can delete your account, and with it withdraw your consent, directly from the app: Profile → "Delete my account". For everything else, write to hello@ffhorizon.studio or ask an administrator of your ward or branch. You can exercise your rights with each of the joint controllers. We reply within one month.

Right to object. You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest (security and moderation), by writing to hello@ffhorizon.studio (Art. 21 of the GDPR).

If you believe your data is not being processed correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (the Italian Data Protection Authority).

10. How we protect the data

  • All communications between the app and the servers are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored in encrypted form.
  • The database applies access rules that separate the data of each ward or branch and of each role: everyone sees only what they are entitled to.
  • There is no open sign-up: you can only join by invitation from an administrator.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with the date of the update. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.
