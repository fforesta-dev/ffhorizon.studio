Delete your account

You can ask at any time to delete your Il Mio Rione account and your personal data. There are three ways to do it.

1. From the app (the quickest way)

  1. Open Il Mio Rione and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap "Delete my account": a page opens that explains what will be deleted.
  3. Tap the red "Delete my account" button.
  4. Confirm with "Delete".

Your account and your personal data are deleted immediately, and this cannot be undone. It works even if your account is suspended.

2. Ask an administrator

The bishopric of your ward (or the presidency of your branch) can delete your account from the app.

3. Write to us

Send an email to hello@ffhorizon.studio with the subject "Il Mio Rione account deletion", from the same email address you use to sign in to the app, and tell us your ward or branch.

We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your account: first name, last name, email and password;
  • ward or branch, location, role, groups, organizations and links to parents or children;
  • the dates on which you gave your consent and accepted the rules of use;
  • your responses to events;
  • your offers for sacrament meeting and the history of when you prayed or spoke;
  • the codes of your phones for notifications and your choices about which ones to receive.

What remains

The notices and events you published for the ward or branch remain visible to the other members, and the reports you sent remain available to the administrators, but all without your name. These are also deleted automatically, within the periods stated in the privacy policy (for example events 12 months after their date). If you want them removed right away, ask an administrator or say so in your email.

Events you added to your phone's calendar with "Add to calendar" are in your calendar, not in the app: if you wish, delete them from there.

The providers' backup copies are overwritten automatically according to their technical schedules. For details, read the privacy policy.

Delete only some data, without deleting your account

You can also ask to delete only part of your data and keep using the app.

  • Your responses to events ("I'm going", "Maybe", "I'm not going"): you can remove them yourself by opening the event and tapping "Remove my answer".
  • Your offers for sacrament meeting: you can withdraw them yourself in the Service tab, with "Withdraw your offer". To remove an entry from the history, ask an administrator of your ward or branch.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off yourself in Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings; when you sign out of the app, the phone code is deleted.
  • Notices and events you published: if you are a leader or an administrator, you can delete them yourself from the app; otherwise, ask an administrator.
  • Groups and role: ask an administrator of your ward or branch to remove them.
  • Any other data: write to hello@ffhorizon.studio with the subject "Il Mio Rione data deletion", from the email address you use in the app, saying which data you want deleted. We do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What remains: first name, last name, email and ward or branch are needed to use the app, so they remain as long as the account exists. To delete these too, delete your account as explained above.
