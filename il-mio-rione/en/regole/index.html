Rules of use

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This is a translation. If there are any differences, the Italian version prevails.

Il Mio Rione is a space for your ward or branch: let's use it with the same respect we show in the chapel. These rules apply to everyone (members, leaders and administrators), and you must accept them to use the app.

1. Who publishes

Notices and events are published only by the administrators of the ward or branch and by the leaders they designate, for their own organization. There is no chat. Other members read, reply "I'm going", "Maybe" or "I'm not going" and can report content.

Help requests (Service tab) are published by the administrators; leaders' requests are published once an administrator approves them. Adults can reply "I can help" with a short message, which only those who manage the request can see.

2. What is not allowed

Zero tolerance for:

  • offensive, vulgar or threatening content, or content that makes fun of someone;
  • discriminatory content, for example based on origin, nationality, sex, age, disability or religion;
  • violent or sexual content, or any other content not suitable for young people;
  • illegal content or content that encourages people to do something illegal;
  • other people's personal data without their permission: addresses, phone numbers, health, family or financial circumstances;
  • advertising, sales, fundraising and political propaganda;
  • pretending to be someone else or using someone else's account.

3. Young people

Minors use the app too, with a parent's permission: all content must be suitable for them. There is no private contact between adults and young people in the app.

4. How to report

At the bottom of every notice, event and help request there is "Report this content": the report goes to the administrators of your ward or branch and to the app manager. You can also write to hello@ffhorizon.studio. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

5. What happens to those who do not follow the rules

The content is deleted and the account may be suspended or deleted, even without warning in serious cases. The app manager can intervene in any ward or branch.

6. Your account

Your account is personal: do not lend it to anyone and do not tell anyone your password.

7. Unofficial app

Il Mio Rione is not an app of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. For personal matters, or if you need help, speak to your bishop or branch president.

8. Changes

If we make significant changes to these rules, we will tell you in the app.
